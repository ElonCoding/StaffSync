# Phase 1: Ideation Phase — Stakeholder Analysis & User Personas

## 1. Stakeholder Matrix

| Stakeholder Role | Primary Objective | Key Requirements | Success Indicator |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **HR Administrator** | Total organizational governance & compliance | Global directory control, company-wide leave oversight, org announcements, full analytics | Zero administrative discrepancies, immediate audit trail access |
| **Team Manager** | Departmental velocity & staff readiness | Subordinate leave approval desk, team attendance telemetry, training track assignment | Reduced approval turnaround time, upskilled team cohorts |
| **Staff Member / Employee** | Frictionless self-service | 1-click check-in/out, instant leave balance visibility, learning curriculum access | High satisfaction, transparent communication |
| **Executive Leadership** | Strategic workforce optimization | Aggregated headcount, attendance consistency, and leave utilization metrics | Decision-ready real-time reporting |

---

## 2. User Personas

### Persona A: Sarah Chen — Human Resources Director
- **Demographics**: 34, Senior HR Professional, oversees 150+ staff across engineering and operations.
- **Goals**: Eliminate spreadsheet sprawl, ensure statutory leave policies are adhered to, deliver company-wide policy updates without email fatigue.
- **Pain Points**: Reconciling leave balances manually, chasing managers for approvals, outdated employee contact records.

### Persona B: David Miller — Engineering Team Lead (Manager)
- **Demographics**: 38, Engineering Manager managing 12 software developers and QA engineers.
- **Goals**: Maintain sprint momentum while respecting employee time-off, identify team skill gaps, monitor daily attendance patterns.
- **Pain Points**: Interruptions by leave requests sent through messaging apps, inability to view who is in the office vs remote in real time.

### Persona C: Alex Rivera — Junior Full-Stack Developer (Employee)
- **Demographics**: 24, Recently joined, hybrid schedule.
- **Goals**: Clock in and out with zero friction, apply for planned vacation leaves, complete required technical and compliance courses.
- **Pain Points**: Complicated corporate HR portals with broken sessions, lack of transparency on leave request status.
